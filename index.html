Signal journey

IDLE
  1. Sample soilADC converts probe voltage to a number.
  2. CalibrateRaw ADC becomes moisture percentage.
  3. Read climateDHT11 reports temperature.
  4. Check waterTank switch protects the pump.
  5. Start ruleDry soil and available water request irrigation.
  6. Stop rule55% moisture prevents relay chatter.
  7. Drive relayD8 energises the isolated pump circuit.
  8. ReportStatus appears on Serial Monitor.
